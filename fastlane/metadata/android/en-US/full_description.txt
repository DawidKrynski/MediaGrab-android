MediaGrab saves videos and photos from links you share to it.

In any app tap Share and pick MediaGrab. The download starts at once, with no window to confirm. A notification shows the progress and lets you cancel. Files are saved to Download/Media/<site>.

* YouTube, TikTok, Instagram reels and many other sites supported by yt-dlp
* Instagram posts and carousels keep every photo and video (needs signing in to Instagram inside the app)
* X posts keep all their photos, videos and GIFs
* Profiles and playlists are refused, so one share never starts hundreds of downloads
* Shared links are downloaded one after another

The app uses yt-dlp, gallery-dl and FFmpeg. yt-dlp updates itself once a day from GitHub, because sites change often.
